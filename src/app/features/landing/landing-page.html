The Dissector

Turn a WhatsApp group chat into an AI-generated forensic-psychology report — archetypes, red flags, superlatives, and a verdict for every person in the chat.

Dissect your chat Run entirely on this device

On-device needs no API key and sends nothing anywhere — at the cost of a one-time model download (from ~650 MB) and a browser with WebGPU.

How it works

  1. Upload — paste or drop your exported WhatsApp .txt file. It's parsed in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
  2. Review participants — a last, calm look at who's about to be profiled. Rename or remove anyone before continuing.
  3. Pick a model — bring your own API key for Gemini, GPT, Claude, Grok or DeepSeek, or run a model on your own machine. No account, no server either way.
  4. Dust for prints — your browser measures the chat first: who dominates, who deflects, who never replies, and the emotional temperature over time.
  5. Consent — with a cloud model, nothing is sent until you explicitly say so, with an option to anonymize names first. On-device, there's nothing to consent to sending.
  6. Get your report — a Group Dynamic Audit, individual dossiers, and superlatives, ready to print or save as a PDF.

What you get

  • Client-side parsing. Your chat is parsed entirely in your browser — with a cloud model nothing leaves your device until you consent, and on-device nothing leaves it at all.
  • Your model, your key — or no key. Gemini, GPT, Claude, Grok and DeepSeek via your own API key, or a model running offline on your own hardware.
  • Evidence, not vibes. Behavioural metrics and flagged quotes are computed on your device first, so the report argues from something real.
  • A structured dossier. Group Dynamic Audit, individual archetypes, strengths, red flags, and superlatives.
  • Consent and anonymization built in. Nothing is sent anywhere until you approve it, with names optional.
